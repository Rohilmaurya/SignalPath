- **P1 — Recovery comparisons are hardcoded and can mislead on uploaded topologies.** `src/lib/risk-engine.ts:417-462` assigns fixed remaining-risk and recovery-time values, and descriptions/tradeoffs name specific services and impacts such as `api-gateway`, checkout rejection, and guaranteed 504s.
